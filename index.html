01 / THE IDEA

Give navigation
a little initiative.

A robot should do more than follow the next command. It should choose a useful way forward, ask when something is unclear, and carry that understanding into its next visit.

navigate_candidate / navigate_pixel / navigate_object / stepAgent chooses · Adapter executes

A movement intention can be expressed as a geometric candidate, a selected floor pixel, an object estimate, or a short discrete action. Actual observations and motion feedback inform the next decision.

Read the abstract+

EVOLIFE connects hybrid actions, active interaction, and persistent spatial memory within one embodied navigation harness. Each encounter can inform the current decision and contribute experience for subsequent tasks.
